SakayNa update: emergency alert call fix

Hi everyone! There's a new SakayNa app. Please update before your next test.

EVERYONE
- Uninstall the old SakayNa app, then install the new one: https://expo.dev/artifacts/eas/0_E9ZASR01BPHCf9-k2DayTdi7U6N_hbO5vbf2zwsOo.apk
- Drivers: nothing changed for you, but please update too.

RESIDENTS
- After a dispatcher answers your emergency alert, your phone still
  calls after a 3-second countdown you can cancel.
- It now calls the dispatcher's own phone. If they don't have one, it
  calls the dispatch office, and the countdown says "Calling the
  dispatch office in 3...".

DISPATCHERS
- Refresh the website.
- The resident always calls you, so the resident's number on the
  Incoming Emergency pop-up and the Active emergency card is now plain
  text (for reference), not a call button.
- After you tap Answer, the resident's phone calls the phone number on
  your account. Please make sure it's correct (ask an admin to check
  Users → Edit). Without one, it calls your Operational phone, then
  the office number.
